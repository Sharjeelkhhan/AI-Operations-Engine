# NovaDesk Refund Policy

## 1. Duplicate Charges
- If two or more successful payments exist for the same subscription period, the duplicate(s) may be refunded.
- Evidence required: At least two successful payment records with the same customer_id and subscription_id within 30 days.
- Refunds above $500 require manager approval.

## 2. Unauthorized Transactions
- Claims of unauthorized transactions must be escalated to the security team.
- No automatic refund is allowed for unauthorized transaction claims.

## 3. Cancellation Refunds
- If a customer cancelled their subscription before the payment date, any subsequent payment is eligible for refund.
- If cancellation occurred after the payment date, the payment is considered valid and no refund is due.
- The cancellation date must be confirmed in the subscriptions table.

## 4. General Refund Conditions
- Refunds are only issued to the original payment method.
- Refund requests must be made within 60 days of the payment date.
- Cases where the customer’s claim cannot be verified with transaction records must be escalated for human review.
