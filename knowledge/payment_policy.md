# NovaDesk Payment Policy

## 1. Payment Processing
- Payments are charged automatically on the renewal date.
- A payment can have status `successful`, `failed`, or `pending`.

## 2. Failed Payments
- If a payment fails, the subscription may be suspended after 3 days.
- Customers reporting a successful payment that does not appear in the system must be investigated manually.

## 3. Payment Records
- All successful payments generate an invoice with status `paid`.
- Failed payments generate an invoice with status `unpaid`.
- Invoices are linked to subscriptions.

## 4. Data Integrity
- Customer claims about payments must be cross-checked against payment and invoice records.
- A single customer statement is not sufficient for automatic action.
